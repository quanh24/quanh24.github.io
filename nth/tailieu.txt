PHAN TICH CONG THUC SAT THUONG PVP
NGHICH THUY HAN MOBILE - PHIEN BAN 2.1.1
============================================================

LUU Y QUAN TRONG VE DO CHINH XAC
-------------------------------
Tai lieu nay tong hop mo hinh cong thuc va cac vi du da trao doi.
Day la mo hinh tham khao theo phan tich cong dong, khong phai cong thuc
ma nguon chinh thuc cua NetEase. Mot so he so trong cac cau tra loi truoc
chua duoc xac minh doc lap day du. Vi vay khong nen xem cac con so duoi
day la cong thuc chinh thuc hoac ap dung chac chan cho moi ky nang.

Can dac biet kiem tra lai:
- He so phong thu 10743.
- He so khang nguyen to 2016.
- Cong thuc Bao kich va Chinh xac.
- Co che Khi thuan / Pha thuan.
- Cach cong don va nhan cac nhom buff.
- He so sat thuong cong trinh khac sat thuong nguoi choi.

1. TONG QUAN CONG THUC
---------------------
Mo hinh tong quat:

D = (D_cong + D_nguyen_to)
    x M_khac_che
    x M_ky_nang
    x M_dac_biet
    x M_bao_kich/do_don

Trong do:
- D_cong: sat thuong tu cong kich sau cac buoc tinh lien quan.
- D_nguyen_to: sat thuong nguyen to sau khang nguyen to.
- M_khac_che: he so khac che phai.
- M_ky_nang: he so rieng cua ky nang.
- M_dac_biet: he so tu noi cong, trang bi, dac chat, buff/debuff.
- M_bao_kich/do_don: he so bao kich hoac do don neu co.

Mo hinh tach hai nhanh sat thuong:

D_cong = (B + K x A_con_lai) x (1 - R_phong_thu)

D_nguyen_to = K x E x (1 - R_nguyen_to) x M_nguyen_to

Sau do nhan cac he so khac neu co.

Ky hieu:
B = sat thuong co so cua ky nang.
K = he so ky nang.
A_con_lai = cong kich sau cac dieu chinh dang so.
E = cong kich nguyen to.
R_phong_thu = ty le giam sat thuong do phong thu.
R_nguyen_to = ty le giam sat thuong do khang nguyen to.

2. CONG KICH, KHI THUAN VA PHA THUAN
----------------------------------
Mo hinh minh hoa cong kich con lai:

A_con_lai = A - S_khi_thuan + C - R_so

Trong do:
A = cong kich.
S_khi_thuan = gia tri Khi thuan con hieu luc sau xu ly Pha thuan.
C = khac che dang so.
R_so = chong chiu dang so.

Khong nen mac dinh Pha thuan tru Khi thuan theo ty le 1:1 trong moi
truong hop. Can xac minh co che va nguong ap dung trong game.

Vi du minh hoa:
Cong kich = 8.000
Khi thuan con lai = 1.000
Khac che dang so = 200
Chong chiu dang so = 100

A_con_lai = 8.000 - 1.000 + 200 - 100 = 7.100

3. PHONG THU VA PHA PHONG
-------------------------
He so phong thu duoc su dung trong mo hinh mua 2.1.1:

R_phong_thu = F_con_lai / (F_con_lai + 10743)

F_con_lai = max(0, Phong_thu_doi_phuong - Pha_phong)

Ty le sat thuong cong kich con lai:
1 - R_phong_thu

Vi du:
Phong thu doi phuong = 8.000
Pha phong = 4.000
F_con_lai = 4.000

R = 4.000 / (4.000 + 10.743) = 27,14%
Ty le sat thuong con lai = 72,86%

Luu y: he so 10743 can duoc doi chieu voi cap nhan vat va phien ban
cu the truoc khi dung de tinh chinh xac.

4. CONG KICH NGUYEN TO VA KHANG NGUYEN TO
-----------------------------------------
Mo hinh:

R_nguyen_to = E_khang_con_lai / (E_khang_con_lai + 2016)

E_khang_con_lai = max(0, Khang_nguyen_to - Bo_qua_khang_nguyen_to)

D_nguyen_to = K x E x (1 - R_nguyen_to) x M_nguyen_to

Vi du:
Khang nguyen to = 3.000
Bo qua khang nguyen to = 2.000
Cong kich nguyen to = 3.000
K = 1

E_khang_con_lai = 1.000
R = 1.000 / (1.000 + 2.016) = 33,16%
D_nguyen_to = 3.000 x 66,84% = khoang 2.005

Luu y: khang nguyen to va phong thu thong thuong la hai nhanh tinh
khac nhau trong mo hinh. Khong tu dong lay phong thu de tinh sat thuong
nguyen to.

5. BAO KICH VA KHANG BAO
------------------------
Gia tri bao kich con lai:

C_con_lai = Bao_kich - Khang_bao

Cong thuc ty le bao kich duoc neu trong phan tich cong dong:

P_bao_kich =
(1,1445 x C_con_lai + 80) / (C_con_lai + 1602,5)

Can gioi han ket qua trong khoang 0% den 100%.

Vi du:
Bao kich = 3.000
Khang bao = 2.000
C_con_lai = 1.000

P = (1,1445 x 1.000 + 80) / (1.000 + 1.602,5)
  = khoang 47,05%

Sat thuong khi bao kich:
D_bao_kich = D_thuong x M_bao_thuong

Vi du minh hoa neu bao thuong = 150%:
D_bao_kich = D_thuong x 1,5

Sat thuong ky vong neu chi co hai ket qua thuong va bao kich:
D_tb = D_thuong x (1 - P) + D_thuong x M_bao_thuong x P

Voi P = 40%, M_bao_thuong = 1,5:
He so trung binh = 0,6 x 1 + 0,4 x 1,5 = 1,2

Luu y: he so bao thuong thuc te co the thay doi boi trang bi, noi cong,
hieu ung va khang bao thuong.

6. CHINH XAC VA DO DON
----------------------
Cong thuc ty le chinh xac duoc neu trong phan tich mua 2:

P_chinh_xac =
(2,3804 x X + 5744) / (X + 6046)

X = Chinh_xac - Do_don_doi_phuong

Can gioi han ket qua trong khoang 0% den 100%.

Vi du:
Chinh xac = 2.000
Do don = 1.000
X = 1.000

P = (2,3804 x 1.000 + 5.744) / (1.000 + 6.046)
  = khoang 98,34%

Luu y: can kiem tra lai cong thuc va gioi han trong ban game dang choi.
Co che Do don va sat thuong sau Do don co the phu thuoc loai ky nang.

7. KHAC CHE PHAI / KHANG PHAI
-----------------------------
Mo hinh he so phan tram:

M_khac_che = 1 + C_phan_tram - R_phan_tram

Vi du:
Khac che phai = +8%
Khang phai = 3%

M = 1 + 0,08 - 0,03 = 1,05

Sat thuong tang 5%.

Phan biet:
- Khac che dang so: co the tac dong vao nhanh cong kich dang so.
- Khang dang so: co the tru vao nhanh cong kich dang so.
- Khac che %: he so tang sat thuong.
- Khang %: he so giam sat thuong.

Can xac minh nhom chi so va thu tu tinh trong tung truong hop.

8. TANG SAT THUONG VA GIAM SAT THUONG
------------------------------------
Neu cac hieu ung thuoc cac nhom nhan doc lap:

M_tong = (1 + Tang_nhom_1)
         x (1 + Tang_nhom_2)
         x (1 - Giam_nhom_3)

Vi du:
Tang sat thuong ky nang = 10%
Tang khac che = 5%
Giam sat thuong muc tieu = 20%

M = 1,10 x 1,05 x 0,80 = 0,924

Sat thuong con 92,4% so voi truoc cac hieu ung nay.

Khong cong tat ca phan tram neu chung thuoc cac nhom nhan khac nhau.
Can xac minh hieu ung nao cung nhom va hieu ung nao khac nhom.

9. VI DU TINH SAT THUONG TONG HOP
--------------------------------
Day la vi du gia dinh de minh hoa cach ghep cac thanh phan.
Khong phai ket qua do trong game.

Nguoi tan cong:
- Cong kich: 8.000
- Khi thuan con lai: 1.000
- Khac che dang so: 200
- Chong chiu dang so: 100
- He so ky nang K: 1,2
- Sat thuong co so B: 2.000
- Cong kich nguyen to: 3.000
- Khac che phai: +5%
- Tang sat thuong ky nang: +10%
- Ty le bao kich: 40%
- Bao thuong: 150%

Nguoi phong thu:
- Phong thu: 8.000
- Pha phong: 4.000
- Khang nguyen to: 3.000
- Bo qua khang nguyen to: 2.000

Buoc 1 - Cong kich con lai:
A = 8.000 - 1.000 + 200 - 100 = 7.100

Buoc 2 - Sat thuong cong kich:
F_con_lai = 8.000 - 4.000 = 4.000
R_phong_thu = 4.000 / (4.000 + 10.743) = 27,14%

D_cong = (2.000 + 1,2 x 7.100) x (1 - 0,2714)
       = khoang 7.666

Buoc 3 - Sat thuong nguyen to:
E_khang_con_lai = 3.000 - 2.000 = 1.000
R_nguyen_to = 1.000 / (1.000 + 2.016) = 33,16%

D_nguyen_to = 1,2 x 3.000 x (1 - 0,3316)
            = khoang 2.406

Buoc 4 - Cong hai nhanh:
D_co_ban = 7.666 + 2.406 = 10.072

Buoc 5 - Nhan khac che va tang sat thuong:
D = 10.072 x 1,05 x 1,10
  = khoang 11.633

Buoc 6 - Sat thuong ky vong co bao kich va chinh xac:
M_bao_kich_tb = 0,6 x 1 + 0,4 x 1,5 = 1,2
Neu xac suat trung = 98%:

D_tb = 11.633 x 1,2 x 0,98
     = khoang 13.681

Ket qua nay chua tinh Do don, buff/debuff rieng, gioi han sat thuong,
he so tung ky nang va cac co che chua duoc xac minh.

10. DANH GIA CHI SO CHO HUYEN CO PVP
------------------------------------
Cac nhom chi so can so sanh:
- Cong kich va cac chi so tang sat thuong.
- Cong kich nguyen to.
- Pha phong va bo qua khang nguyen to.
- Bao kich va bao thuong.
- Chinh xac.
- Khac che phai.
- Sinh ton: phong thu, Khi thuan, khang nguyen to.

Khong co thu tu uu tien co dinh cho moi nhan vat. Gia tri cua tung chi so
phu thuoc muc tieu, cap doi thu, ky nang, noi cong va buff dang su dung.
Huong dan build Huyen Co PvP 2.1.1 co the dung de tham khao, nhung khong
nen ap dung bang xep hang cua mot phai cho tat ca phai.

11. SAT THUONG NGUOI CHOI VA SAT THUONG CONG TRINH
-------------------------------------------------
Khong nen dung nguyen cong thuc danh nguoi cho sat thuong pha tru.

Danh nguoi PvP co the tinh:
- Phong thu nguoi choi.
- Khang nguyen to.
- Khi thuan / Pha thuan.
- Bao kich / Do don.
- Khac che phai.
- Buff va giam sat thuong.

Pha tru can kiem tra rieng:
- He so sat thuong cong trinh.
- Ky nang nao tac dong len cong trinh.
- Buff tang sat thuong cong trinh.
- Co che phong thu / trang thai cua cong trinh.
- Gioi han sat thuong va thoi gian hoi chieu.

KET LUAN
============================================================

1. Sat thuong cong kich -> Khi thuan/Pha thuan -> Phong thu/Pha phong.
2. Sat thuong nguyen to -> Khang nguyen to/Bo qua khang.
3. Nhan cac he so khac che, ky nang, buff/debuff.
4. Xet Bao kich, Chinh xac va Do don.
5. Kiem tra rieng co che sat thuong cong trinh.

Cac he so 10743, 2016 va cac cong thuc Bao kich/Chinh xac trong tai lieu
la cac gia tri tham khao tu phan tich cong dong cho mua 2.1.1, can duoc
kiem chung bang thu nghiem trong game truoc khi su dung lam cong cu
tinh toan chinh xac.
